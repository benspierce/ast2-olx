Contributors
============

Course
======

Wesam Salem, instructor of ELED 3261 (Elementary Science Methods) at the
University of Memphis, is the author of the course abstract and the
learning goals this activity is built on, and directed revisions to its
content.

Scenario and courseware
=======================

The design document was prepared by Benjamin Pierce from the course
abstract. The scenario, characters, and OLX in this repository were
developed by Piotr Mitros, and revised by Benjamin Pierce after review
with the course instructor in the fall of 2026.

Platform
========

This activity runs on lo-blocks. The platform, as well as the Learning
Observer project it is part of, and the Open edX platform they are based
on, were originally conceived of and created by Piotr Mitros. Platform
contributors are listed at https://github.com/olxhub/lo-blocks/.
